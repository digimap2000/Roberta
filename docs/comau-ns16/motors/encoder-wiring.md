## Motor Encoder Colour Coding
| Signal | Wire Colour | Default 15-Pin Pin |
| :----: | :---------: | :----------------: |
| `VCC`  |     RED     |         2          |
| `GND`  |    WHITE    |         3          |
| `EA+`  |    BLACK    |         1          |
| `EA-`  |    BLUE     |         13         |
| `EB+`  |   YELLOW    |         11         |
| `EB-`  |    GREEN    |         12         |
Motors from StepperOnline with built in encoders come with 15 pin RS232 connectors. The internal wiring is coloured as per the table above and connects directly to the motor controller inputs.
